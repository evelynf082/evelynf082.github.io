Here's a little bit about me

You can usually find me practicing flute, painting, or spending far too long making handmade gifts for friends.

I design CAD, hardware, and electrical systems for my FIRST Tech Challenge robotics team.

Outside of robotics, I teach STEM through libraries and community programs, helping younger students explore engineering and design.

Lately, I've been researching molecular motors, creating my own design for a nanoscale cargo-sorting robot out of DNA origami.
