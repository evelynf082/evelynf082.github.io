Geoengineering Paper

2026 · Science communication

I wrote this paper during the third semester of my junior year, spending over 25 hours writing and editing, and over 10 hours diving into articles. As I said, I knew absolutely nothing about geoengineering before beginning my research, but the topic immediately caught my attention. I learned so, so much during this process---from drawing parallels with history, to current efforts and future predictions. While it was certainly a difficult process (with many ups and downs) it was incredibly rewarding to shine a light on a topic that previously was completely opaque to me. I hope you find my essay similarly illuminating.

Major Challenges

I think my biggest challenge was just the sheer volume of work that I had to complete. I read over 30 articles and wrote 14 pages. To say that writing this essay consumed all of my thoughts is an understatement.

Another challenge I had was writing with voice. My AP Lang teacher pushes her students to show their voice through their writing. Initially, I was hesitant to show my perspective as a high school student when this pressing issue has nothing to do with me. I, as the author, am simply a vessel for sharing this information. However, with much encouragement, I slowly began to add more and more of my opinions into the work. I know this may not be the traditional, formal research paper that someone might find in an academic journal. It certainly does get a bit unhinged at points. But when you read my writing, it's almost as if I'm directly talking to you. It's authentic, and that's what I love most about it.
